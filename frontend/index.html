How it works

Three simple steps to get started.

1. Register / Login

Students sign up with their details. Officers use credentials issued by the placement cell.

2. Practice & Share

Attempt aptitude tests, review answers, and submit interview experiences for approval.

3. Track Progress

Students see personal growth; officers monitor the entire batch and moderate content.
